Frequently Asked Questions

What's free vs. paid?

The core Tier 2 and Tier 3 pension calculators are always free. Unlocking Full Access adds the Final Withdrawal Planner (including the reverse target-pension solver), Deferred Compensation modeling, and the Accuracy Check statement scanner.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed through your Apple ID and managed in Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone. Cancel anytime at least 24 hours before renewal to avoid being charged for the next period.

How do I restore a purchase on a new device?

Open the Unlock Full Access screen in the app and tap "Restore Purchases." This restores any Lifetime, Annual, or Monthly purchase tied to your Apple ID.

Is this financial or legal advice?

No. This app provides planning estimates only. It is not financial or legal advice, and using it does not create any advisory relationship. Always confirm final figures with your pension fund.

Something looks wrong with my calculation — what do I do?

Email us the details (screen you were on, the inputs you used, and what looked off) and we'll take a look.
