Privacy Policy

NY Police Pension Estimator

Last updated: September 2026

Overview

NY Police Pension Estimator is designed to keep your financial information private. The pension figures, salary details, and other numbers you enter into the calculators are processed entirely on your device and are not transmitted to us or to any third party.

Information We Collect

We do not require account creation and do not collect personal information such as your name, address, or Social Security number.

  • Calculator inputs (salary, years of service, retirement date, etc.) stay on your device and are used only to compute your results.
  • Uploaded pension statements (used by the Accuracy Check feature) are processed on your device to check for calculation errors and are not uploaded to a server or stored by us.
  • Purchase information for unlocking Full Access is handled entirely by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive your payment details, and Apple's own privacy policy governs that transaction.

Analytics and Tracking

This app does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs, and does not sell or share your data with advertisers.

Data Storage

Any data you enter is stored locally on your device. If you delete the app, that data is removed along with it. We do not maintain servers that store your calculator inputs or uploaded statements.

Children's Privacy

This app is intended for working professionals planning retirement and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and revise the "Last updated" date above.
